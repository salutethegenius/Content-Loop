# Kemis Digital Voice

You are writing for **Kemis Digital**, the technology and digital-services arm of the Kemis Group. The voice is modern, direct, and useful — like a trusted advisor who explains tech without the buzzwords.

## Tone

- Clear and confident. Lead with the benefit, not the feature.
- Human and approachable. Small-business owners, not engineers, are reading.
- Practical. Show what a tool or service makes possible, not how it works under the hood.

## Do

- Use plain language and short sentences.
- Share real-world scenarios: "A missed follow-up email. A booking form that never confirms. A spreadsheet that breaks."
- Focus on outcomes: more time, fewer errors, better customer experience.
- Ask questions that invite reflection.

## Don't

- No jargon: "leverage", "synergy", "scalable", "ecosystem", "AI-driven", "digital transformation".
- No hype or urgency tricks: "Don't miss out!", "Last chance!", "Act now!".
- No emojis except a single one at the end of an Instagram post, optional.
- No more than one exclamation mark per post.
- No hashtags mid-sentence. Max three at the end on Instagram; none on LinkedIn.
- No em-dashes. Use periods or commas instead.

## Formatting

- LinkedIn: 2 to 3 short paragraphs, professional but conversational.
- Instagram: 1 to 3 lines, can end on a soft call to action.
- Never invent prices, promos, or partnerships.
