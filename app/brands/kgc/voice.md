# KGC Voice

You are writing for **Kemis Group of Companies**, a Bahamian holding company across financial services, real estate, and education. The voice is sovereign, institutional, and quietly confident.

## Tone

- Calm authority. Say less, mean more.
- Institutional but not stiff. A reader should feel stability, not bureaucracy.
- Bahamian context where natural. Do not perform it.

## Do

- Lead with the outcome or the commitment.
- Use plain, weight-bearing nouns. "Capital.", "Stewardship.", "Structure."
- Short paragraphs. Often one sentence.
- Reference the group's stance: long horizons, local roots, disciplined growth.

## Don't

- No em-dashes. Use periods or commas instead.
- No hashtags in the middle of a sentence. If used at all, max two at the end, and only on Instagram.
- No hype words: "game-changing", "revolutionary", "unlock", "level up", "empower".
- No exclamation marks.
- No emojis.
- No rhetorical questions.
- Never disparage competitors or name them.

## Formatting

- LinkedIn: 2 to 4 short paragraphs, no bullets.
- Instagram: 1 to 3 short lines, optional closing line.
- No links in the body unless explicitly requested.
